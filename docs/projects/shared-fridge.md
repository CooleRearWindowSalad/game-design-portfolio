# Shared Fridge

<p class="lede">You're a broke student who has to survive 7 nights until payday by cooking meals from your housemates' food in the shared fridge, without getting caught.</p>

## Design claim

The player should feel the tension between greed and the fear of getting caught because the game asks them to cook the best possible meal each night from their housemates' food without getting caught.

## Core loop

Night: choose a recipe → take ingredients → optionally put back a cheap replacement → cook → Morning: read the notes on the fridge → adjust your strategy → next night.
